RemindMe

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

1. Introduction

RemindMe is a productivity application designed to help users organise reminders, manage schedules and stay organised. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the application.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, RemindMe may process:

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Reminder titles, descriptions, dates, times and recurrence settings.
  • Reminder completion and scheduling information.
  • Voice notes created using supported application features.
  • Information required for cloud synchronisation.
  • Technical and advertising information processed by third-party services.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Provide reminder and scheduling functionality.
  • Deliver notifications and reminder alerts.
  • Support account authentication.
  • Synchronise supported information through cloud services.
  • Maintain application security and reliability.
  • Display advertisements where permitted by applicable privacy choices.

4. Cloud Services and Supabase

RemindMe uses Supabase for account authentication and supported cloud functionality. Information submitted to these services may be processed and stored by Supabase.

For more information, read the Supabase Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

RemindMe uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising interactions and other technical information, subject to applicable law and your privacy choices.

RemindMe integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent where required. The application also provides a Privacy Options entry point for reviewing available privacy choices when applicable.

Read more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Information

6. Notifications

RemindMe may schedule local notifications to deliver reminders. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

7. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored locally on your device or processed through supported third-party services, depending on the features you use. Reasonable measures are used to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as necessary to provide application functionality and maintain supported services, subject to applicable legal requirements.

For requests concerning account deletion or associated cloud information, contact the developer using the email address provided below.

Uninstalling the application may not delete information that has already been synchronised to cloud services.

9. Children's Privacy

RemindMe does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws. Where required, appropriate parental consent and privacy protections must be applied.

10. Third-Party Services

RemindMe uses third-party services, including Google AdMob, Google UMP and Supabase. These providers process information under their respective privacy policies and applicable terms.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this webpage.
