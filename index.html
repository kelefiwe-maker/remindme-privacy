Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

1. Introduction

RemindMe helps users organise reminders, manage schedules and stay productive. This policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when using the application.

2. Information We Process

  • Account and authentication information.
  • Reminder titles, descriptions, dates, times and recurrence settings.
  • Information used for supported cloud synchronisation.
  • Voice notes created through the application.
  • Technical and advertising information processed by third-party services.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide reminders, notifications, account access, cloud synchronisation, application security, reliability and advertising where permitted.

4. Cloud Services

RemindMe uses Supabase for account authentication and supported cloud services. Information handled by Supabase is subject to its privacy practices.

Supabase Privacy Policy

5. Advertising

RemindMe uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, advertising interactions and other information according to applicable settings and consent.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to manage consent where required. Users can access Privacy Options in the application when available.

Google Advertising Information

6. Notifications

The application may schedule local notifications to deliver reminders. Notification permissions can be managed through device settings.

7. Data Security

We use available security measures to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as necessary to provide the application, maintain accounts and meet applicable obligations. Users may contact the developer to request account or associated data deletion.

Uninstalling the application may not delete information already synchronised to cloud services.

9. Children's Privacy

RemindMe does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

10. Third-Party Services

The application uses third-party services, including Google AdMob, Google UMP and Supabase. Their respective privacy policies explain how they process information.
